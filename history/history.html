The history of identification.

Identification systems have changed as transportation, government administration, printing technology, security needs and digital systems have evolved.

Identification is a technology story, too.

Modern identification documents combine policy, identity data, printing, security features and increasingly digital systems. Looking at their history helps explain why documents contain the fields and features they do today.

Evolution of driver’s licenses

Driver licensing developed as motor vehicles became more widespread and governments established rules around who could operate them. Over time, licenses became more standardized and increasingly served as widely used photo identification.

History of U.S. identification

The United States does not have one universal national identity card for everyday use. Instead, identity is established through a collection of federal, state, tribal, institutional and other credentials designed for different purposes.

REAL ID history

The REAL ID Act was enacted in 2005. It established federal minimum standards for state-issued driver’s licenses and identification cards for certain federal purposes. Implementation has taken place over time through federal rules and state programs.

Evolution of passport security

Passports have incorporated increasingly sophisticated security and machine-readable features. Modern travel documents use standardized formats and multiple security elements intended to make documents more resistant to alteration and easier for border systems to process.

Digital identification

Digital identification can refer to systems that represent or verify identity electronically rather than relying solely on a physical credential. Digital credentials, mobile identity documents and online identity verification are developing in different jurisdictions and use cases.

Technology changes quickly. For current requirements, acceptance rules or implementation dates, use the relevant government agency or issuing authority as the source of truth.
